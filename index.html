About

We build software that behaves less like a product and more like a space.

Most software is assembled from parts that never met. We start at the foundation and work upward, so the data, the logic and the screen behave as one thing. People feel that as calm, speed and trust.

Discipline
Product engineering, interface design, platform infrastructure
Working model
One small senior team from first sketch to production
Location
Remote, working across time zones

Anatomy of a system

Four layers.
One structure.

  1. 01

    Interface

    What people touch. Design systems, interaction, motion and the front end, tuned until nothing needs explaining.

  2. 02

    Logic

    Product rules, services and APIs. The part that has to be right every time, so we test it like it matters.

  3. 03

    Data

    Models, pipelines and search. We instrument from day one so the numbers show whether the work is working.

  4. 04

    Infrastructure

    Cloud, delivery and monitoring. Built to stay quiet until the moment it is needed.

Process

Four stages, each one standing on the last

  1. Weeks 1 to 2

    Frame

    We map the problem, the users and the constraints, then agree on what the first release has to prove.

    • Problem brief
    • Success measures
    • Risk map
  2. Weeks 3 to 5

    Prototype

    A working prototype in front of real users. Cheap to change, expensive to ignore.

    • Clickable prototype
    • Five user sessions
    • Scope decision
  3. Weeks 6 to 16

    Build

    Weekly releases to production. The people who designed the system also write it.

    • Weekly releases
    • Automated test suite
    • Runbooks
  4. Ongoing

    Operate

    Monitoring, support and steady improvement. We stay until your team is ready to take over.

    • Round-the-clock monitoring
    • Monthly review
    • Handover plan
